## Before you hit post

- **First comment:** drop the hashtags there instead of the caption if you'd rather keep it clean — works the same either way.
- **Alt text** (Advanced settings → Write alt text): "Carousel listing five beginner-friendly vegetables: cherry tomatoes, lettuce, peppers, green beans, and basil and mint, with days to harvest for each."
- **Link in bio** must be live before posting — `sprouttogether.app`.
- **Reply to every comment** in the first hour. On a new account that's most of your reach.

## Why this one first

A brand-new account has no audience, so post #1 has to earn a stranger's
attention on its own. This one is saveable (saves are the strongest ranking
signal), it's useful before anyone cares who you are, and the app only shows
up on slide 7 — after the value has landed. "Meet Sprout Together" is post 3
or 4, once there's someone there to meet you.
