Start posting (build the habit)

Consistency beats perfection. Follow the weekly rhythm near the bottom of this page.

Ongoing · ~20 min/day
  • Set your cadenceInstagram: 3–4×/week, Reels first. Pinterest: 5–10 pins/week.
  • Schedule a week of posts at onceUse Later/Buffer on Sunday to queue the week — then just engage.
  • Do 15 minutes of engagement dailyReply to every comment; comment on 5–10 other gardeners' posts.

4

Get discovered

Go where gardeners gather. Lead with genuine help — never spam a link.

Week 2+ · a few hours/week
  • Join & help in gardening communitiesReddit (r/gardening, r/vegetablegardening), Facebook groups. Mention the app only when it genuinely fits.
  • Reach out to 5–10 micro-influencersGardeners with 1k–50k followers. Offer free Pro access for an honest mention or story.
  • Ask happy users for App Store reviewsBoosts ranking + conversion. Add an in-app review prompt in a future build.
  • Submit to app roundups & Product HuntSearch “best gardening apps” lists. Many feature a free, polished app for the asking.
5

Turn on paid ads

Once your listing converts and you have ~$300/month to test, start with Apple Search Ads — cheapest, highest-intent channel for apps.

When ready · ~$10/day to start
  • Set up Apple Search AdsAt searchads.apple.com: a “Discovery” campaign (Search Match on) + an “Exact keywords” one. Keywords below.
  • Start at $10/day, optimize weeklyRun 3–4 days untouched. Then raise bids on cheap converters, add wasteful terms as negatives; keep cost-per-install under ~$2–3.
  • Later: test Meta & Pinterest adsReuse your best organic posts as the ad creative.
