# Reels you can film off your own app + what to post next

Everything here is filmable with iOS screen recording (Control Centre → Record) on a
real device. No talking head required. Keep each under 20 seconds — completion rate is
the single biggest lever on Reels reach.

**Audio note:** you're on a Business account, so you get Instagram's *commercial*
music library, not the full trending catalogue. Don't fight it — promoting a paid app
with trending pop audio risks the audio being muted or the post pulled. Pick something
upbeat from the licensed library, or use no music and let the taps carry it. Text-driven
Reels with no audio do fine.

**Hook (on screen, first 2 seconds):** `POV: you stop guessing where things go`

**Shots**

## Reel 4 — "Everything basil needs in 10 seconds"

Fast, satisfying, endlessly repeatable — remake it for any plant. Make this your series.

**Hook:** `Basil, explained in 10 seconds`

**Shots:** one slow scroll down the Basil detail page, top to bottom. That's it.

**On-screen text:** let the app's own headings do the work. Add only the hook and an end card.

> Repeat for tomato, garlic, strawberry, mint. Same format, new plant. Series content
> trains the algorithm on who to show you to, and it costs you two minutes each.

**Pinterest runs on a different clock.** Pin 2–3 times a week, and re-pin the same
image to different boards a few days apart — that's normal there, not spam. Your
companion cheat sheet will keep working for months; Instagram posts are gone in 48 hours.

**The habit that matters most:** reply to every comment in the first hour. On a new
account, early engagement decides how far a post travels — it outweighs anything you
do afterwards.
