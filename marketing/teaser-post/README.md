# Coming soon — plant scanner teaser

**Instagram only.** No Pinterest pin for this one: Pinterest is a search engine
and nobody searches for a feature that doesn't exist yet. A teaser there would
sit unseen. Pinterest gets the *problem* post instead (`problems-post/`), and
the scanner announcement once it ships.

---

## Two deliberate choices

**It says "part of Sprout Together Pro."** Teasing a feature and revealing the
paywall at launch generates exactly the comments you don't want. Saying it now
costs a little reach and buys goodwill.

**The last line asks a question.** Replies on a teaser tell you which problems
people actually want solved — useful for the launch caption, and comments in
the first hour drive how far the post travels.
