## Part B — App Store screenshot overlays

The bold text you put *over* each screenshot. Do the first 3 especially well — they show in search results.

| # | Headline (big) | Subhead (small) |
|---|----------------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Care guides for 154 plants** | Veggies, herbs & flowers — all in your pocket |
| 2 | **Never miss planting season** | Know exactly when to plant & harvest |
| 3 | **Design your garden beds** | Lay out plots and plants in minutes |
| 4 | **Companion planting, solved** | See what grows well together |
| 5 | **Track every season** | Photos, notes & progress in your journal |

### How to make them in Canva (10 min)
1. New design → **Custom size → 1290 × 2796 px** (iPhone 6.7").
2. Background: your **dark green** (`#35543a`). Place the **app screenshot** in the lower ~70%, leaving a header band on top.
3. In the top band: the **headline** in bold pale-mint/white, the **subhead** smaller beneath it. Keep text in the **top third** so it's readable as a thumbnail.
4. Add your small **sprout logo** in a corner for consistency.
5. Use the **same font, colors, and layout on all 5** so they read as a set.
6. Export as PNG → upload in App Store Connect (same place as the description).

> 🎨 Keep it simple and high-contrast. One headline per screenshot. Pale text on the dark green reads great at thumbnail size.
